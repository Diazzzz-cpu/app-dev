# My Favorite Anime Series: Overlord

![Overlord Banner](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/7/7a/Overlord_II_Key_Visual.jpg)

---

## About the Series
**Overlord** is a Japanese *dark fantasy* anime based on the light novel written by **Kugane Maruyama**.  
The story follows *Momonga*, a gamer who finds himself trapped inside his favorite MMORPG, **Yggdrasil**, when the servers shut down.  
In this new reality, he becomes **Ainz Ooal Gown**, the powerful overlord of the Great Tomb of Nazarick.

---

## Main Characters
- **Ainz Ooal Gown** – The undead sorcerer and main protagonist.  
- **Albedo** – Overseer of the Guardians, completely devoted to Ainz.  
- **Shalltear Bloodfallen** – A vampire guardian known for her unpredictable nature.  
- **Demiurge** – A demon strategist with high intelligence.  
- **Cocytus** – A loyal insectoid warrior who values honor.

---

## Why I Like Overlord
> "In a world where power defines destiny, Ainz proves that intellect and strategy can rule all."

1. It gives a **unique villain perspective** rather than the usual hero story.  
2. The world-building is detailed and immersive.  
3. The characters are complex and morally gray.  
4. The mix of fantasy, action, and politics keeps the story engaging.
